# Fantasy synopses

## 1. Street Netrunner

You are a freerunner for hire in Metroplex. Jobs come from a fixer board:
exfil a badge, scrub a camera log, kill a watcher process, crack a payroll DB.
Style is neon, illegal, fast. The city OS is hostile; every district is a
permission domain. Best fit for "cool cyberpunk fantasy" and combat-adjacent
metaphors (`kill`, firewalls, implants).

## 2. Corp IT Mole

You work helpdesk / SRE for Helix Dynamics — or you *look* like you do.
Jobs are tickets: rotate a cert, restore from git, query the HR database,
quiet a runaway process before the pager escalates. Humor and bureaucracy
over street violence. Same skills, different fiction. Great for people who
already work in IT and want the learning to feel like work (in a good way).

## 3. Open-Source Resistance

You are part of the Archive Collective. Corps hoard knowledge behind ACLs
and encrypted volumes; your missions liberate docs, repair git history of
censored repos, and expose public truth via queries and scripts. Closest in
spirit to Tuxville’s “Order of the Open Source,” but modern and political.
Strong teaching narrative for ethics and why tools matter.
